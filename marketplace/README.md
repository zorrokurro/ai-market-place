## 🧭 What is AgentOS?

AgentOS is the **Steam for AI Agents & Workflows**.

Just as Steam didn't just sell games — it built *discovery, trust, community, and ecosystem* — AgentOS is not just an AI directory. It's the **decision layer** between humans and an exploding world of AI agents.

| What others do | What AgentOS does |
|---|---|
| Listing | **Comparison** |
| News feed | **Workflow showcase** |
| Generic recommendations | **Structured evaluation** |
| AI directory | **Decision Engine** |
